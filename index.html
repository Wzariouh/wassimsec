WassimSec

A personal publishing tool for short educational videos about cybersecurity.

What this is. WassimSec is a self-hosted desktop application. It turns a written script into a vertical short video (voice-over, diagrams and captions) and publishes it to the operator's own TikTok account through TikTok's official Content Posting API.

How it works

  • The operator writes the script of a short video about online safety.
  • The app generates the video locally on the operator's computer.
  • The operator authorizes their own TikTok account with Login Kit (Desktop, PKCE).
  • The app uploads the finished video to that same account with the Content Posting API.

Who uses it

Only the account owner. There is no sign-up, no third-party users, no user-generated content from the public and no advertising. The app is not distributed as a service.

Scopes used

  • user.info.basic — read the display name of the authorized account.
  • video.upload — upload the generated video file.
  • video.publish — publish the video to the authorized account.
