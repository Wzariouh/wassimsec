Privacy Policy

WassimSec · Last updated: 17 November 2026

Short version: the app runs on the operator's own computer. It does not collect, sell or share personal data from anyone. The only data it handles is the TikTok authorization token of the account owner.

1. Who this policy covers

WassimSec is a private desktop application used by a single operator. It has no visitors, accounts or customers, so no personal data from third parties is processed.

2. What data the app handles

  • TikTok OAuth token (access and refresh token) issued by TikTok Login Kit after the owner authorizes the app.
  • Basic account information returned by the user.info.basic scope: the account's display name and username.
  • The video files generated locally and uploaded to the owner's account.

3. Where the data is stored

Tokens are stored only on the operator's computer, in the user's private configuration folder (~/.config/canal-tiktok/) with file permissions restricted to the owner. No server, database or cloud storage is used.

4. What the app does not do

  • No analytics, tracking pixels, advertising or profiling.
  • No collection of data from other TikTok users or from website visitors.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of data with third parties.
  • No reading of private messages, followers or content the owner did not create.

5. This website

These pages are static HTML. They do not set cookies, do not load external resources and do not collect access logs for marketing purposes.

6. Data retention and deletion

Tokens are kept until the owner revokes access. Access can be revoked at any time from the TikTok app (Settings → Security and permissions → Manage app permissions) or by deleting the local folder ~/.config/canal-tiktok/. Once access is revoked, the app can no longer act on the account.

7. Children

The app is not directed at children and is not distributed to them.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated.
